1. Executive Summary

The model contains two separate price routes. The Consumer Strategy Route produces a POCAS first-offer price and a required final consumer SRP while enforcing an 8% POCAS gross-margin floor. The SKW Comparison Route starts with SKW's current discounted retail price and reports the resulting POCAS margin exactly as calculated, with no floor.
1Four-stage channel: POCAS → Wholesaler → Retailer → Consumer.
2Cost basis: NJ LANDED COST = P/U COST × 1.05.
3Consumer strategy: preserve 30% wholesaler GM and 40% retailer GM; raise POCAS offer and final SRP only when base POCAS GM is below 8%.
4SKW comparison: use the current discounted SKW retail price to show the actual no-floor POCAS GM.
5Do not mix the routes: MAX MARGIN SRP does not include SKW retail prices.
68% means gross: after the current 2% commercial allowance, an 8% gross GM is approximately 6% modeled net GM.

3. How SKW Discounted Retail Price Relates to Landed Cost

Management distinction: the SKW discounted retail price is not calculated from NJ Landed Cost. The two are independent inputs. Their direct economic relationship appears only after the SKW consumer price is reverse-calculated into an implied POCAS offer and compared with NJ Landed Cost.

Cost-side input

POCAS's modeled NJ cost basis starts with the SKW B2B purchase price.

NJ LANDED COST
= P/U COST × (1 + 5%)

Market-side input

The current discounted SKW retail price is reverse-calculated through the two channel margins.

POCAS OFFER (SKW)
= SKW SRP × 60% × 70%
= SKW SRP × 42%

POCAS margin at SKW price

The two independent inputs meet in the margin calculation.

POCAS GM (SKW)
= [POCAS OFFER (SKW) − NJ LANDED COST]
÷ POCAS OFFER (SKW)

Effect of a deeper SKW discount

If SKW lowers its consumer price while B2B cost remains unchanged, the implied POCAS offer falls and POCAS GM declines or becomes negative.

Lower SKW SRP
→ Lower implied POCAS Offer
→ Lower POCAS GM

Executive price thresholds

POCAS gross break-even consumer price Landed Cost × 2.381 Landed ÷ (70% × 60%) = Landed ÷ 42%

If the SKW consumer price is below this level, POCAS GM is negative under the 30%/40% channel structure.

Consumer price required for 8% POCAS gross GM Landed Cost × 2.588 Landed ÷ (92% × 70% × 60%) = Landed ÷ 38.64%

This is the approximate consumer price required to preserve POCAS 8%, wholesaler 30%, and retailer 40% gross margins.

Gross versus net: the workbook's 8% POCAS floor is before the 2% wholesale commercial allowance. Under the current formula, an 8% gross GM becomes approximately 6% modeled net GM after allowance. If management requires 8% after allowance, the gross floor must be separately increased and approved.

5. Worked Example: MECUS14-TP

Product: Medicube Kojic Acid Turmeric Pad, 70 pcs

Cost and market inputs

SKW B2B P/U Cost$13.18
NJ Landed Cost$13.839
displayed ≈ $13.84
Reference MSRP$31.00
Realistic Market SRP$24.80
Verified Consumer SRP$24.80
SKW Discounted SRP$26.40
SKW Retail Original$33.00
SKW Discount$6.60 / 20%

Consumer Strategy Route

MAX MARGIN SRP $31.00
→ Base W→R $18.60
→ Base POCAS Offer $13.02
→ Base POCAS GM −6.29%

The base GM is below 8%, so the floor is applied.

POCAS Offer $15.05
→ POCAS Gross GM 8.05%
→ Final W→R $21.51
→ Required Consumer SRP $35.85
  • Wholesaler GM: approximately 30.03%
  • Retailer GM: 40.00%
  • SRP increase: $4.85 / approximately 15.6%
  • POCAS modeled net GM after 2% allowance: approximately 6.05%

SKW Comparison Route

SKW Consumer Price $26.40
→ SKW W→R $15.84
→ POCAS Offer (SKW) $11.08
→ POCAS GM (SKW) −24.90%

At the current SKW discounted consumer price, the implied POCAS offer is below the approximately $13.84 landed cost.

  • No 8% floor is applied in this route.
  • The negative GM is a market-economics warning, not a formula error.
  • Required consumer price for the 8%/30%/40% structure: $35.85.
  • $35.85 is $9.45, or approximately 35.8%, above the current SKW price.
What this example means: a recognized brand or strong-selling product may still fail to support POCAS wholesale economics when the combination of SKW discounted retail price and SKW B2B cost is unfavorable.

6. Executive Decision Guide

What You SeeWhat It MeansRecommended Review
AC ≥ 8% and AH/AI = 0The strategic SRP supports POCAS, wholesaler, and retailer economics without an SRP increase.Revalidate cost, source, inventory, and resale authority before prioritizing.
AC ≈ 8% with a large AH/AIThe POCAS floor works mathematically, but the required consumer-price increase is significant.Review consumer acceptance, competing prices, brand MSRP/MAP, and channel fit.
AM < 0The current SKW discounted retail price cannot support POCAS cost while preserving 30% wholesaler and 40% retailer margins.Negotiate lower B2B cost, approve a channel-margin change, position at a higher SRP, or hold launch.

AR = Single Evidence or Research RequiredMarket-price support is incomplete.Complete price research before quoting.
AZ is not Exact MatchThe source may refer to another size, pack, shade, or version.Stop using the price until the exact product is confirmed.

8. Glossary and Operating Cautions

TermMeaning
SRPSuggested Retail Price; the consumer selling-price reference.
GMGross Margin; revenue less cost, expressed as a percentage of revenue.
P/U CostPrice per unit paid to the B2B vendor.
NJ Landed CostCurrent modeled NJ cost basis: P/U Cost plus a 5% uplift.
First OfferPOCAS's opening supply-price proposal to the wholesaler; it is not automatically a final approved quote.
Wholesaler→Retailer PriceThe price at which the wholesaler supplies the retailer.
FloorA minimum margin or price that the model will not go below.
Commercial AllowanceModeled deduction for promotion, support, or other wholesale commercial costs.
No FloorThe calculated result is displayed without forcing it up to a minimum margin.

Operating cautions

  • This workbook is a pricing-strategy and negotiation model. It is not automatically a final quote or purchase-order price.
  • SKW Retail prices can change through promotions, time deals, or coupons. Recheck the retail source and checked date immediately before quoting.
  • P/U Cost and B2B inventory may have different verification dates by row. Reconfirm the signed-in B2B price and stock before purchasing.
  • MAX MARGIN SRP uses only columns M:O. It excludes SKW SRP and [SKW] RETAIL ORIGINAL.
  • A negative POCAS GM AT FIRST OFFER (SKW) may be an accurate economic warning, not a formula problem.
  • Do not issue an external quote until exact SKU, size, pack, version, resale authority, MSRP/MAP, inventory, seller, and ships-from party have been verified.
One-sentence conclusion POCAS should treat SKW B2B cost plus the 5% uplift as its landed-cost basis, reverse-calculate channel prices from both the strategic consumer SRP and the current SKW discounted retail price, and then compare the implied POCAS offer with landed cost. The Consumer Strategy Route enforces an 8% gross-margin floor; the SKW Comparison Route intentionally shows actual market economics with no floor.
